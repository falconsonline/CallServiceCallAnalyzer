2 types of Sigtran association
a) M2PA
	Our Node: IP Address, SPC
	Adjacent Node: IP Address, SPC (Direct Sigtran assoication)
	Remote Node:  SPC
	
	Our node can be determined by 2 details
	1) Our node IP Address (denoted by CNSYS). Each Sigtran nodes have 2 IPs for redundancy.
	CNSYS:IPADDR=172.26.131.2,IPADDR2=172.26.131.18,PER=0,AUTOACT=N;
	In this example 172.26.131.2 and 172.26.131.18 are our node IPs.
	2) Our Node SPC:
	SNSLI:SNLINK=1,IPADDR=172.26.133.2,PPORT=5000,SS7MD=ITU14,HPORT=4087,SNTYPE=M2PA,M2PA=1,SNEND=S;
    SNSLI:SNLINK=2,IPADDR=172.26.132.2,PPORT=5000,SS7MD=ITU14,HPORT=4087,SNTYPE=M2PA,M2PA=2,SNEND=S;
	Denoted by SNSLI:SNLINK. This also tells if Sigtran association is M2PA or M3UA.
	Here SNLINK=1 is Linkset#1 and 
	SNLINK=2 is Linkset #2
	Peer node IPs and ports as mentioned by IPADDRand PPORT. There can be multiple IP addresses to the Adjacent Node   
	SPC of adjacent nodes are denoted by  MTP_LINKSET
	e.g.
	* MTP_LINKSET <linkset_id> <adjacent_spc> <num_links> <flags> <local_spc> <ssf>
	MTP_LINKSET    0            6508              1       0x0001     7001      0x00
	MTP_LINKSET    1            6371              1       0x0001     7001      0x00
	
	Here linkset_id 0 equals to SNLINK value 1 and linkset_id = SNLINK=2 and adjacent_spc accordingly and so is local_spc.
	Remote SPCs are identified by MTP_ROUTE
